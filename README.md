## 🌟 What is AquaQuest?

AquaQuest is a mobile-first Progressive Web App designed to make freshwater citizen science easier, more understandable, and more engaging for everyday communities.

The platform brings together:

- 🗺️ Water-site exploration
- 🔎 Guided environmental observations
- 📸 Photo-supported evidence
- 🚩 Environmental concern reporting
- 📖 Community water stories
- 🤝 Community challenges
- 🏆 Gamified participation
- 📚 Environmental learning
- 📊 Water-health indicators
- 👤 Personal contribution and impact tracking

Instead of treating citizen science as a complicated data-entry process, AquaQuest turns it into a guided experience:

**Explore → Observe → Understand → Contribute → Act**

---

## 💡 Why AquaQuest?

Urban freshwater ecosystems are affected by pollution, litter, biodiversity loss, urbanization, and other environmental pressures.

People living around these environments often notice changes before formal monitoring systems do — unusual water colour, visible litter, wildlife sightings, blocked waterways, unusual smells, or other local changes.

The problem is that these everyday observations are often:

- Unstructured
- Difficult to report
- Disconnected from other community observations
- Hard for non-experts to interpret
- Not connected to meaningful community action

AquaQuest is designed to bridge that gap.

It gives citizens a simple way to observe what they see, understand what it may mean, contribute evidence, and participate in collective action.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Citizen science tools can become difficult for ordinary users when they rely on technical terminology, complicated workflows, or disconnected monitoring systems.

AquaQuest focuses on five practical problems:

1. **Observation is difficult to structure**
   Users may notice environmental changes but not know what information is useful.

2. **Environmental terminology can be intimidating**
   AquaQuest uses simplified categories such as water clarity, litter, smell, colour, wildlife, and surrounding conditions.

3. **Reporting can feel disconnected**
   A report should not simply disappear after submission. Users need feedback, community participation, and visible impact.

4. **Community participation needs sustained engagement**
   Challenges, contribution tracking, points, levels, badges, and achievements are used to encourage continued participation.

5. **Environmental information should be understandable**
   AquaQuest presents site-level health indicators and One Health explanations in a format intended for everyday users.

## 💡 Our Solution

AquaQuest connects the citizen science journey into one mobile experience.

**Explore**

Discover freshwater and aquatic sites through a map and water-site directory.

**Observe**

Record structured observations through a multi-step guided workflow.

**Evidence**

Attach photographs to observations and environmental reports.

**Report**

Report concerns such as heavy litter, unusual water colour, strong smells, dead wildlife, wastewater discharge, or blocked waterways.

**Understand**

View environmental indicators and educational content explaining what observations may mean.

**Participate**

Join community challenges and contribute toward collective environmental goals.

**Track**

Monitor water sites, view personal activity, collect achievements, and see contribution progress.

## 💾 Prototype Data Architecture

AquaQuest currently operates without a production cloud backend.

Instead, it uses a mock service/API layer combined with local persistence.

The service layer simulates backend-style operations for:

- Authentication
- Users
- Water sites
- Observations
- Contributions
- Challenges
- Reports

This means the frontend communicates through structured service functions rather than depending directly on individual UI components for data operations.

**Current storage**

```
↓

```

**Future production architecture**

```

```

## ⚠️ Transparency Note

### This is a Functional Frontend Prototype

AquaQuest is intentionally a prototype rather than a production environmental monitoring platform.

The current version uses:

- Mock data
- LocalStorage persistence
- Simulated service/API responses
- Prototype authentication
- Client-side application state
- Demonstration community content

There is currently no production cloud database or real-time multi-user synchronization.

This allows the prototype to demonstrate the complete citizen science experience without requiring a production backend during the hackathon.

The architecture was designed so that future backend services can be introduced without rebuilding the entire interface.

## 🧪 Prototype Design Principles

AquaQuest was developed around several principles:

**Simple enough for non-experts**

Environmental participation should not require specialist terminology.

**Mobile first**

The primary interaction model is designed around small screens and touch interactions.

**Evidence-based**

Photos and structured observations make community contributions more useful.

**Action-oriented**

Observation should lead toward understanding and action rather than becoming an isolated submission.

**Community-driven**

Environmental stewardship becomes stronger when observations and actions are shared.

**Honest prototyping**

The current system clearly separates prototype functionality from future production infrastructure.
